Welcome to Neighbourhood Connect

Neighbour Connect is a community hub designed to bring people in the same neighborhood closer together. It’s a place where residents in Lagos-Surulere can share updates, discover local events,and support one another.

With Neighbour Connect, you can stay informed about what’s happening around you, connect with people who live nearby, and find helpful resources within your area.

"Neighbour Connect is a fantastic platform that has truly transformed our community. It has made it so much easier to connect with my neighbors, share important updates, and organize local events." - Boluwatife Words

Things you can do on Neighbour Connect

  • Connect with neighbors
  • Share local updates
  • Buy/sell items(The marketplace)
  • Organize events
  • Share safety alerts
  • Get help quickly
